Privacy Policy

How we tend to your data with the same care we give our gardens.

Last updated: April 18, 2026

At Uncharted Garden Paths, we believe that privacy is a fundamental part of tending a thriving community. This policy outlines what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — all in plain language, without the legalese thicket.

🌿 Our promise: We never sell your personal data. We use analytics only to understand how our readers engage with stories, and we keep your information secure.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide a thoughtful reading experience and to improve our content.

📬 Newsletter sign‑upName & email address (only if you opt in).
🍪 CookiesSmall files that remember your preferences and help us understand site usage.
📊 AnalyticsAggregated data about page visits, referrers, and device types (via privacy‑friendly tools).
💬 CommentsName, email, and IP address (to prevent spam) when you leave a comment.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send you our newsletter – if you have subscribed, we share occasional garden stories, seed notes, and seasonal tips.
  • To improve our site – anonymous analytics help us understand which articles resonate most.
  • To respond to inquiries – if you reach out via email or contact form, we use your details to reply.
  • To prevent spam – IP addresses and comment data help us keep the conversation civil.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal, first‑party cookies to enhance your experience. You can manage your cookie preferences in your browser settings. We do not use third‑party advertising cookies.

  • Essential cookies: Keep you logged in (if applicable) and remember your cookie consent.
  • Analytics cookies: We use a self‑hosted analytics setup (or privacy‑respecting service) that does not track you across other sites.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers (e.g., email delivery) who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements. We never share data for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: If anything is incorrect, let us know and we’ll update it.
  • Erasure: You can ask us to delete your personal data (except where we are required to keep it for legal reasons).
  • Unsubscribe: Every newsletter contains an unsubscribe link, or you can email us directly.

To exercise these rights, just drop us a line at hello@unchartedgarden.com.

6. Data Security

We protect your data with industry‑standard measures: encrypted connections (SSL), limited access, and regular security reviews. While no system is 100% secure, we take every reasonable precaution.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our content is intended for a general audience; we do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date. For significant changes, we may also notify you via email or a notice on the site.

📬 Questions? We're here to help. Reach out anytime at hello@unchartedgarden.com.
