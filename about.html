About this garden.

Uncharted Garden Paths is an independent publication rooted in curiosity, heirloom wisdom, and the quiet art of tending living things. We believe every seed holds a story.

Our compass

We explore the forgotten, the overlooked, and the deeply rooted — from Appalachian heirlooms to soil microbiology, from pollinator corridors to the poetry of growth.

Founded in 2021, Uncharted Garden Paths began as a small newsletter among friends and has grown into a quiet almanac for gardeners, naturalists, and anyone who finds solace in the soil. We are ad‑free, independent, and committed to regenerative practices.

🌱 no‑dig 🧑‍🌾 seed saving 🐝 native plants

📖 Heirloom preservation

We document and celebrate rare varieties — from Cherokee Purple tomatoes to Jacob’s Cattle beans — and connect with seed keepers around the world.

🌿 Soil as living system

We champion no‑dig, compost, and fungal networks. Healthy soil is the foundation of flavor, resilience, and carbon capture.

🪶 Wild edges & pollinators

We advocate for native plants, hedgerows, and insect habitat. A garden is not an island; it is part of a larger ecology.
