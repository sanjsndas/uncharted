Cookies Policy

A bite-sized guide to the digital crumbs we leave behind.

Last updated: April 18, 2026

At Uncharted Garden Paths, we use cookies to make your visit as pleasant as a stroll through a well-tended garden. This policy explains what cookies are, why we use them, and how you can manage them.

🌱 Our approach: We use only essential and performance cookies. We do not use any third-party advertising or tracking cookies. Your privacy is paramount.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device when you visit. They help the website remember your actions and preferences (like login details or language) over time so you don't have to re-enter them each time.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

🔐 Essential CookiesRequired for the site to function properly (e.g., remembering your cookie consent choice).
📊 Analytics CookiesHelp us understand how visitors interact with our site, using privacy-first tools like Plausible or self-hosted analytics.
⚙️ Preference CookiesRemember your settings, such as font size or theme preferences (if applicable).
📬 Session CookiesTemporary cookies that expire when you close your browser; used to maintain your session.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. We may occasionally embed content (e.g., YouTube videos) that sets its own cookies, but we do not control these. We encourage you to check the privacy policies of those services.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Here's how to manage cookies on popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our site.

5. Consent

When you first visit our site, we ask for your consent to use non-essential cookies. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies or adjusting your browser settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect any changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
